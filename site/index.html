What counts

Frontier model trainers only. Tier 1 resets the counter: harm at scale from a shipped model, exposed user data, a governance or safety-team collapse, deception, an internal process that permitted the harm, or large-scale misuse of the company's model. Tier 2 is logged without resetting.

Lawsuits never reset it, at any stage. The counter tracks what these companies did, not what they were sued for. Nor do capability announcements, funding, pricing, or departures without a stated safety reason. Incidents are dated to first public disclosure.

Where an incident involves a death, a child, or abuse imagery, the jokes come off: no sign, no swear.

The full rubric, the code, and every entry are in the repository. If something here is wrong, open an issue with a source and it gets corrected.
